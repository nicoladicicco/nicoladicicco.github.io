I am an Operations Research Specialist at [OPTIT](https://optit.net), where I
work on decision support systems. I focus on the logistics domain, especially on three-dimensional packing and
vehicle routing problems, and I contribute to the architecture of our
cloud-based software platform.

Previously, I obtained a PhD in Information Technology at
[Politecnico di Milano](https://www.polimi.it) under the supervision of Prof.
[Massimo Tornatore](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=50-RA6kAAAAJ&hl=en). During my PhD, I was an
[NGI Enrichers](https://enrichers.ngi.eu) fellow at
[Columbia University](https://www.columbia.edu) under the supervision of Prof.
[Gil Zussman](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=-E5k6m4AAAAJ&hl=en), and an intern at [IIJ Research Laboratory](https://www.iijlab.net/en/)
under the supervision of Dr. [Jean-François Baffier](https://baffier.fr).

I enjoy spending time with my family, reading, listening to Japanese city pop, playing videogames, parrots, etc. You know, the usual stuff.
